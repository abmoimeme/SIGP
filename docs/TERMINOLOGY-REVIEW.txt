SIGP — Senegal terminology review, 7 November 2026

CHANGED
- Internal status names distinguish completion of unit work from judicial resolution. Existing stored statuses migrated consistently.
- Assignment uses Affectée; supervisor action is an internal data check, not a legal condition for receiving a complaint.
- Printed document is an Accusé de saisie — démonstration, specimen without official value; no assertion of statutory receipt issuance.
- Main courante renamed Registre de service pending unit validation. Lost/found routing is a demonstration convention, not asserted Senegalese law.
- Anonymous entry described as Signalement sans identité rather than an anonymous nominative complaint.
- Pieces described as an internal inventory, distinct from judicial seizure, scellés, or transfer to a registry.
- Police national directorate profiles excluded from the fictional brigade selector; brigade uses its command wording. Full Gendarmerie hierarchy remains to design and validate.
- Reference panel explains OPJ/APJ status, plaignant/declarant/temoin, persons implicated vs convicted, notes vs procès-verbaux, and judiciary vs administrative hierarchy.
- Task deadlines remain internal; no custody, limitation, retention or transmission deadline is computed.
